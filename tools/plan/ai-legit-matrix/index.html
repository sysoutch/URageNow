Quick read

Generally safer: using your own material, public-domain/openly licensed material, or AI for description, accessibility, summarization, or analysis without copying protected expression. Riskier: generating close substitutes for copyrighted/licensed characters, extracting text from images you do not own, rebuilding models/assets from someone else’s image, or using outputs commercially without checking rights.
